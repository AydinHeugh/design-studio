# Job: new from scratch

**Keeps:** nothing.
**Changes:** everything: structure, look, motion, voice.

**Confirm with the user:** "Starting from scratch: nothing is fixed. Is there anything you want kept anyway (a logo, colors, a name, content, a layout you like)?" Anything they name becomes frozen.

**Directions differ in:** layout, or identity on one strong layout. Pick the axis the brief needs and say why.

**Watch for:** content that isn't settled yet. New projects often start without real text and images; run the content check in the interview and label directions as demos if content is missing.
