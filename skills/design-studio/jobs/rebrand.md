# Job: rebrand

**Keeps (frozen):** layout and structure, flows, navigation, content and what everything does.
**Changes:** identity: color, type, imagery, icon style, voice and tone, motion personality, logo use.

**Confirm with the user:** list the specific pages and elements you'll keep and the identity parts you'll change. Ask if any should move between the lists. Ask whether a new logo is in scope (if yes, it should come from them or a designer; you only place it).

**Directions differ in:** identity on the same layout.

**Watch for:** a new identity that breaks readability or contrast on existing layouts; check every screen type, not just the home page.
