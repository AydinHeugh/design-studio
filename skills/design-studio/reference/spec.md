# Spec: DESIGN.md (step 7)

Write `DESIGN.md` in the project, or update the existing one. Every section is short.

```
# DESIGN.md
## Job: job, and the confirmed keep / change lists
## Thesis: one sentence: what this is and the one real thing it shows
## Readers: who they are, what they need first, how they use it (in the user's words)
## Match: how the look and motion come from what the product does
## Tokens: 4-6 named colors (+ states for UI); type families, scale, weights; one spacing scale; radius and elevation by role
## Layout: structure, grid, alignment, max widths, how it collapses on a phone
## Use: where it's used, and what that sets (minimum type size, density, contrast)
## Visuals: every image, diagram, chart and icon, each with its job. No job, no visual.
## Look order: what the eye hits 1st, 2nd, 3rd on each key screen, and what makes each win (size, contrast, position, motion)
## Components and states: default, hover, pressed, focus, disabled, loading, empty, error
## Motion: trigger, properties, duration, easing, exit, reduced-motion fallback (numbers from the emil pack)
## Acceptance: the checks in critique.md
```

Light: show a 3-line summary. Moderate: show it. Deep: review it together.
