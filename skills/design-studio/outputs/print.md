# Output: print and PDF (handouts, plans, guides)

**Packs:** taste, copy, assets, dataviz. No motion.

## Rules
- **Works printed in black and white.** Never rely on color alone; use labels, weight or patterns.
- **Margins** at least 12mm (0.5in).
- **Type:** body at least 10-11pt for reading at a table; at least 14pt for anything read at arm's length or mid-activity (gym, kitchen, workbench).
- **Light backgrounds.** No full dark pages.
- **Never break** a table row, step or exercise across pages.
- **Page numbers** and a short running header.
- **One topic per page** when it's used mid-task.
- **Images at print resolution.**
- **Check a grayscale preview** before calling it done.

## Stack
HTML + CSS print styles to PDF, or the user's tool (Canva, InDesign, Word).
