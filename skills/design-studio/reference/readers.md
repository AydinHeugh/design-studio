# Readers

Readers are the people who will use or read the design, not the person running this skill. **Don't assume who they are or how they think. Ask.** The user's answers become this project's rules.

## Ask
1. **Who reads or uses this?** (Role, how much they already know.)
2. **What do they need first** when they open it?
3. **What are they doing at the time?** (At a desk with time, on a phone between things, under pressure, learning something new.)
4. **What makes them trust it, and what would make them distrust it?**
5. **Anything they hate** in similar products or reports?

Write the answers into `DESIGN.md` under Readers, in the user's words. Design to them. If the user doesn't know, say so in the spec and design for the stated purpose; don't invent a persona.

## Defaults (use only where the user hasn't said otherwise; they can override any)
- Fewer choices at once.
- Related things grouped; the same things in the same place every time.
- Details on request, not all at once.
- Nothing invented: no fake data, quotes, sources or urgency.
