# Changelog

## v0.6 (2026-10-26)
- **Taste setup** on first run: quick rounds of real sites, fonts, palettes and motion. It asks what you like and why; it doesn't test whether you can spot AI.
- **Taste blocks:** Claude stops and asks for any font, color or style choice you haven't settled.
- **Picking sheets:** visual type, color, effects and library boards instead of lists.
- Every landing-page direction needs a moving moment **and** something to click, drag or swipe.
- **Top product sites** and **quiet brands** added as reference types.
- **Content check** before designing.
- The pack check is now shown, with install commands.

## v0.5 (2026-10-23)
- **Rule zero:** the design matches what the product is. "Cover the logo" became the first critique check.

## v0.4 (2026-10-23)
- **Look order:** the plan says what the eye should hit first, second and third; the critique checks it.

## v0.3 (2026-10-23)
- Always asks the depth question (v0.2 skipped it for short prompts; found in the bakery test).
- Every reply shows which step it's on.
- **Motion budgets:** quiet for app UI, a full motion identity for landing pages, portfolios and games. New `motion` pack and `game` preset.
- Directions for moving pages are shown live, not on a static canvas.

## v0.2 (2026-10-23)
- **Find the real project first** (v0.1 designed the wrong thing).
- Depth picker: Light, Moderate, Deep.
- Review Board, Frankenstein references, layout-vs-identity directions.
- Craft vs taste: experts supply craft; taste comes only from the user.
- Asset registry, Kole Jain's rules, a rule for when the user is away.

## v0.1 (2026-10-20)
- First version: phases, pack registry, priority order, DESIGN.md template, coach mode, personal tells from a blind test.
