# Source: Mobbin, "What makes a dashboard stick" (from 2,108 dashboards)

Distilled in our own words. Credit Mobbin.

## Rules
- **Show what to do, not just what happened.** Put the key insight next to the chart (Faith). Weekly recaps and daily briefs beat raw charts (Origin).
- **Package results as highlights** that give a sense of accomplishment (Runner after a run).
- **Pick a visual that is the data itself:** a live globe where every dot is a real visitor (Shopify), a heat map of busy hours (Front), a cake inside an ownership donut (Cake).
- **Clean stands out,** because most dashboards are busy (Visitors).
- **Empty can be a reward.** Paid every bill? Mercury shows a small game instead of an empty table.
- **Coin your own metric.** Streaks (Duolingo), readiness (Oura), closing your rings (Apple). People adopt the word, then they don't switch products.
- **Make it shareable:** screen-time data as swipeable stories (Opal).
- **AI answers "why did this change?"**, from one prompt under a chart to a full assistant beside the data. AI dashboards get about 2× the saves on Mobbin.
- **Gut check:** "Would I screenshot this, and why?" The memorable ones knew what to leave out.

## Tells
- A dashboard that could be any template: hierarchy, spacing and charts all fine, and nothing you'd remember.
