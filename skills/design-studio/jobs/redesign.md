# Job: redesign

**Keeps (frozen):** content and goals (what the page or app is for).
**Changes:** layout and identity. Flows and navigation only if the user explicitly includes them.

**Confirm with the user:** "I'll keep your content and goals, and rethink layout and look. Should flows and navigation also change, or stay as they are?" List what's in scope.

**Directions differ in:** layout.

**Watch for:** dropping content during the redesign. Every piece of existing content needs a place, or an explicit "remove" from the user.
